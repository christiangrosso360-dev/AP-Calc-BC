Practice exam.

The real format, the real timing, and no feedback until you're done — because that's the part everyone skips. At the end your multiple choice is scored, your written free-response answers are graded against the rubric, and you get a predicted 1–5.

  • College Board publishes each unit's share of the exam as a range, not a fixed number. Every exam here is built to a fresh blueprint sampled inside those ranges — so no two attempts are the same shape, and both are legal.
  • Questions you've already been given are held back from your next exam until the bank runs low, so sitting several in a row keeps giving you new material.
  • Calculator sections are marked. Trust the flag: use one only where it says you may.
  • You can move freely within a section, but once a section's time is up it closes.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser — a refresh won't lose your exam.

Score bands follow the widely used composite scale. It's an estimate, not a promise.
